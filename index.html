Application information

Clear rules for connected accounts.

alethia is a first-party automation tool for managing authorized Facebook Page and, where configured, Instagram publishing workflows through Meta APIs.

It is operated for accounts the operator is authorized to manage. alethia does not offer public registration or paid subscriptions.

About alethia

Purpose-built publishing

The application can read permitted account details, submit authorized content, and keep local records of publishing attempts and results.
