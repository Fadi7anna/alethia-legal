Data deletion

Disconnect alethia from Meta first, then contact the operator to request review and deletion of applicable records kept by the local automation.

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Disconnect alethia in Meta

Sign in to the Facebook account that authorized the integration. Open Facebook settings and find Business Integrations (the menu name or location may change). Select alethia and remove or disconnect it. If the integration was granted through Meta Business Settings, also review the connected apps and assigned Page or Instagram assets there.

If a linked Instagram account has its own connected-app authorization, review the relevant Instagram or Accounts Center settings and revoke alethia’s access there as well.

2. Revoke any remaining permissions

Review the permissions and business-asset assignments granted to alethia in Meta settings. Remove any remaining access for the relevant Facebook Page or linked Instagram professional account. If you no longer control the authorizing account, ask the account or business administrator to revoke access.

3. Request deletion of app-held data

Contact the operator using the public project issue page. State that you are requesting deletion of alethia-held data and identify the connected service in general terms. Do not post access tokens, passwords, private messages, or unnecessary personal or account identifiers publicly. Ask for a private contact channel if the operator needs more information to verify or locate a record.

The operator will review the request manually and remove applicable local credentials, publishing-ledger entries, or reports that can be identified and are under the operator’s control. There is no automatic deletion process or fixed local retention period in the reviewed materials. The operator can confirm what was handled through a private channel when one is arranged.

4. What disconnection does and does not do

Disconnecting alethia in Meta revokes authorization for future API access. It does not automatically delete local credentials, reports, or publication records. A separate request to the operator is needed for applicable local data.

Meta controls information stored on Facebook and Instagram, including published posts and Meta account records. Deleting alethia’s local records does not delete content or history held by Meta. Use Meta’s own account and content controls for information stored on its platforms.

5. Meta deletion callback

The materials reviewed for alethia do not contain a server endpoint that receives Meta data-deletion callbacks. This page provides manual instructions and can be used as a data-deletion instructions URL where Meta offers that option. If the configured Meta product or use case requires a dedicated callback URL, that server-side callback must be implemented and configured separately; this static website does not provide one.
